## 9.3 Onboarding

Multi-step wizard:
- **Step 1:** Basics (age, gender, height, weight, activity)
- **Step 2:** Goal (lose / maintain / gain / eat healthier)
- **Step 3:** Diet preferences
- **Step 4:** Targets (calculated, with manual override)
- **Step 5:** Ready screen

### States

2. Image selected
3. Analyzing state
4. AI result
5. Edit nutrition modal
6. Saved success

### Analyzing UI
- Show image preview
- Animated abstract scanning overlay
- Text: "Analyzing your meal…"
- Show steps:
  - Detecting foods
  - Estimating portions
  - Matching nutrition data
  - Preparing summary

## 9.6 AI Result Page

### Show
- Uploaded image
- Meal title generated by AI
- Calories
- Macro breakdown
- Detected food items
- Confidence level
- Assumptions
- Buttons:
  - Confirm
  - Edit
  - Re-analyze with note

### Example

**Detected:**
- 2 boiled eggs
- mixed greens
- olive oil dressing
- tomato
- cucumber

**Assumptions:**
- Dressing estimated as 1 tbsp
- Portion size estimated from plate area
- No hidden sauces detected
