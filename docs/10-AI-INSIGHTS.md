# 10 — AI Insight Examples

Reference examples for the tone, length, and content of AI-generated insights.

---

## Daily

- "You are close to your calorie target and protein is on track."
- "Fiber is low today. Adding fruit, vegetables, or legumes may help."
- "Your meal was calorie-dense, mostly from fats. That is okay, but keep dinner lighter if your goal is weight loss."

---

## Weekly

- "You hit your protein target 4 out of 7 days."
- "Your weekends are higher in calories than weekdays."
- "Breakfast is consistent, but dinner portions vary a lot."

---

## Monthly

- "Your average daily intake decreased by 8% compared to last month."
- "You tracked 22 days this month."
- "Your most common meals were eggs, rice, chicken, and salad."

---

## Tone Guidelines for All Insights

- Encouraging, never judgmental
- Specific (numbers, days, items) over vague
- Always actionable when possible
- No medical claims
- Frame estimates as estimates
- One or two sentences per insight, never paragraphs
